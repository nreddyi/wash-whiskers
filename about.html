Our story

Born from one very wet dog.

Wash Whiskers started with a beagle who hated baths and a family tired of soaked bathrooms, scratched arms and the wet-dog smell.

Where it began

Clean shouldn't mean stressful.

Bath day was a battle. Our beagle would hide, shiver and sulk, and our hands cracked from harsh shampoos. We started asking groomers and vets whether a pet really needs water to get clean.

Mostly, it doesn't. Gentle plant cleansers, the right absorbent powders and a good towel can lift dirt and odour while protecting the coat's natural oils.

So we made our own waterless formulas, tested on human skin first so they're kind to your hands, and never tested on animals.

— The Wash Whiskers family
