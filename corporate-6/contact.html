Ask a Question ↗
Which pets are Wash & Whiskers products for?

Our range is made for dogs and cats. Some products, like the Fruit Shampoo, Pet Powder and Pet Soap, are for both, while others are made specifically for dogs or for cats.

What is the difference between the Grooming and Hygiene ranges?

The Pet Grooming Range cares for your pet's coat and skin: shampoos, dry baths, paw cream, powders and soaps. The Pet Hygiene Range covers paws, ears and the home: cleaners, odour and training sprays, floor cleaner and detergent sheets.

Can I freshen my pet between baths?

Yes. Dry Bath Foaming Shampoo, Dry Bath Spray, Refreshing Deodorising Spray and Pet Powder are designed to refresh the coat between regular baths, without rinsing.

Are your shampoos paraben and sulphate free?

Our dog shampoos are paraben free and sulphate free, and many products use natural extracts such as neem, aloe vera, oatmeal and chamomile. Check each pack for full details.

Do you help with odours around the home?

Yes. Kennel Wash, Floor Cleaner, Pet Area Freshener Spray and Urine Odour Neutraliser Spray are made to keep pet spaces fresh.
