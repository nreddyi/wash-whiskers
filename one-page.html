How it works

Bath time, minus the bath.

No tub, no towel-drying marathon and no wet-dog smell. Three calm steps, about five minutes.

01

Spray or foam

Apply a few pumps of foam or mist onto the coat, focusing on dirty or smelly spots.

02

Massage in

Work it through to the skin with your fingers or a mitt. Plant cleansers lift dirt and oil.

03

Towel off

Rub with a dry towel and brush through. Done: soft, fresh and fluffy, with no rinse.

Our story

Born from one very wet dog.

Bath day was a battle. Our beagle would hide, shiver and sulk, and our hands cracked from harsh shampoos. So we asked groomers and vets whether a pet really needs water to get clean.

Mostly, it doesn't. Gentle plant cleansers, absorbent powders and a good towel lift dirt and odour while protecting the coat's natural oils. Every formula is tested on human skin first and never tested on animals.

FAQs

Good questions.

How can a pet get clean without water?

Our foams and mists use mild plant-based cleansers that attach to dirt and oil. When you towel off, the dirt comes away with the product. Powders absorb oil and odour and are brushed out.

Is it safe if my pet licks the product?

Yes. All formulas are lick-safe and free from parabens, sulphates, harsh alcohol and essential oils that are toxic to cats.

Will it irritate my hands?

No. Every product is dermatologically tested on human skin, so there's no stinging, dryness or sticky residue. You don't need gloves.

Are your products tested on animals?

Never. Wash Whiskers is 100% cruelty-free, and we avoid ingredients that harm animals or waterways.

Does waterless care replace baths completely?

For most pets it greatly reduces the need for wet baths. Very muddy dogs or pets with medical skin conditions may still need occasional bathing as advised by your vet.

From what age can I use the products?

Our Puppy & Kitten range is suitable from 8 weeks of age. For the rest of the range, we recommend 12 weeks and up.
