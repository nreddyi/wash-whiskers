Our Approach

A fresh pet in four easy steps

No tub, no hose and no shivering pets. Just a calm routine that takes about five minutes.

01

Apply

Pump foam or mousse onto the coat, focusing on soiled or smelly spots.

02

Massage

Work it in with your fingers or a mitt. Plant cleansers lift dirt and oil.

03

Towel Off

Rub with a dry towel and brush through. The dirt comes away with the product.

04

Fresh & Fluffy

Conditioning botanicals leave the coat soft, shiny and smelling clean.
