Who We Are

Clean, calm and happy dogs and cats, without a single drop of water.

Wash Whiskers develops waterless grooming for dogs and cats. Our rinse-free foams, mousses, powders and wipes lift dirt and odour while protecting the coat's natural oils, with no tub, no stress and no wet floors.

Every formula is tested on human skin first, so it's kind to the hands that groom. It's also lick-safe for pets and never tested on animals.

Our Approach

A fresh pet in four easy steps

No tub, no hose and no shivering pets. Just a calm routine that takes about five minutes.

01

Apply

Pump foam or mousse onto the coat, focusing on soiled or smelly spots.

02

Massage

Work it in with your fingers or a mitt. Plant cleansers lift dirt and oil.

03

Towel Off

Rub with a dry towel and brush through. The dirt comes away with the product.

04

Fresh & Fluffy

Conditioning botanicals leave the coat soft, shiny and smelling clean.
