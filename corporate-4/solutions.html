Our Approach

A fresh pet in four easy steps

No tub, no hose and no shivering pets. Just a calm routine that takes about five minutes.

01

Apply

Pump foam or mousse onto the coat, focusing on soiled or smelly spots.

02

Massage

Work it in with your fingers or a mitt. Plant cleansers lift dirt and oil.

03

Towel Off

Rub with a dry towel and brush through. The dirt comes away with the product.

04

Fresh & Fluffy

Conditioning botanicals leave the coat soft, shiny and smelling clean.

▶

Why Waterless

Better for pets, easier for you

No Stress

No running water, slippery tubs or fear. Grooming feels like petting.

Saves Water

A full bath can use 40–80 litres. A waterless groom uses none.

Skin-Friendly

Less frequent wet bathing helps protect natural coat oils.

Anytime, Anywhere

At home, in the car or right after a muddy walk.

Questions pet parents ask

Ask a Question ↗
How can a pet get clean without water?

Our foams and mousses use mild plant-based cleansers that attach to dirt and oil. When you towel off, the dirt comes away with the product. Powders absorb oil and odour and are brushed out.

Is it safe if my dog or cat licks the product?

Yes. All formulas are lick-safe and free from parabens, sulphates, harsh alcohol and essential oils that are toxic to cats.

Will it irritate my hands?

No. Every product is dermatologically tested on human skin, so there's no stinging, dryness or sticky residue.

Does waterless care replace baths completely?

For most dogs and cats it greatly reduces the need for wet baths. Very muddy dogs or pets with skin conditions may still need occasional baths, as advised by your vet.

From what age can I use the products?

Our puppy and kitten lines are suitable from 8 weeks of age. For the rest of the range, we recommend 12 weeks and up.

Are your products tested on animals?

Never. Wash Whiskers is 100% cruelty-free, and we avoid ingredients that harm animals or waterways.
