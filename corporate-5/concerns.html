Common Concerns

What would you like to solve?

Choose a concern to see the Wash & Whiskers products made for it, for dogs and for cats.

01

Shedding & Loose Fur

Fur on the sofa and hairballs in cats. De-shedding care loosens the undercoat so it brushes away.

Recommended
Dog: Coat Mist →Cat: Coat & Skin →Cat: Grooming Mitts →
02

Bad Odour

That 'doggy smell' between baths. Waterless foams and dry baths neutralise odour at the source.

Recommended
Dog: Foam Shampoo →Dog: Dry Bath →Cat: Litter & Home →
03

Itchy or Dry Skin

Scratching and flaky patches, especially in winter. Soothing oat and chamomile calm the skin.

Recommended
Cat: Calm Skin Mist →Dog: Aloe Mist →
04

Muddy Paws

Monsoon walks and muddy floors. Foaming paw cleanser cleans between the toes in a minute.

Recommended
Dog: Paw Care →Dog: Grooming Wipes →
05

Tear Stains

Reddish-brown stains under the eyes. Gentle saline pads fade stains over time.

Recommended
Dog: Eye Care →Cat: Bright Eye Pads →
06

Bad Breath

Plaque and doggy breath without the brushing battle. Waterless dental foams and gels help.

Recommended
Dog: Dental Foam →Cat: Dental Gel →
07

Mats & Tangles

Knots in long coats. Detangling mists and dry shampoo keep fur separated and smooth.

Recommended
Dog: Detangler →Cat: Dry Shampoo →
08

Bath-Time Anxiety

Shivering, hiding and scratching at bath time. Rinse-free care makes grooming feel like petting.

Recommended
Cat: Silent Mousse →Dog: Foam Shampoo →
09

New Puppy or Kitten

Little ones shouldn't be bathed too early. Tear-free, ultra-mild formulas from 8 weeks.

Recommended
Puppy Care →Kitten Care →
