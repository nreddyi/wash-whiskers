Who We Are

Clean, calm and happy dogs and cats, without a single drop of water.

Wash & Whiskers develops waterless grooming for dogs and cats. Our rinse-free foams, mousses, powders and wipes lift dirt and odour while protecting the coat's natural oils, with no tub, no stress and no wet floors.

Every formula is tested on human skin first, so it's kind to the hands that groom. It's also lick-safe for pets and never tested on animals.

  • Zero water needed
  • Hand-safe formulas
  • Lick-safe for pets
  • 100% cruelty-free
